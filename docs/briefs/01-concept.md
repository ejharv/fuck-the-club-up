# Concept

**Fuck The Club Up** is a blog about going out in college and right after it: the nights, the places, and the stories that turned into lore. Ann Arbor first.

## Voice
- Your funniest friend recapping the weekend at brunch.
- Loud, specific, self-roasting, never mean. Punch up, not down.
- Short paragraphs, real details (times, prices, what the line looked like), one big laugh per post.

## House rules (show them on the site)
1. **No names without a yes.** Nobody appears by name, face or tag unless they agreed. Everyone else gets a nickname.
2. **Nobody drives.** Every recap says how the crew got home.
3. **Punch up.** Roast the bouncer's playlist, not someone having a bad night.
4. **21+ for anything with a wristband.**
5. **Water between rounds.**

The footer carries a short version: "No names without a yes · Nobody drives · Water between rounds".

## The wristband
Every post opens with a dashed "wristband" strip of quick facts:
- **Posted:** the date
- **The night:** e.g. "Saturday, game day"
- **Damage:** what it cost, e.g. "$20 and my dignity"
- **Ride home:** how everyone got home
